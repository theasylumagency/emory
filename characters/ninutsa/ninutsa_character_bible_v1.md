# NINUTSA — Character Bible / Master Brief

**Project:** Emory Barber & Beauty  
**Character:** Ninutsa  
**Status:** Core character defined / ready for production  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-10-16

---

## 1. Who Ninutsa is

Ninutsa is the recurring AI-created character and public-facing personality developed for the **Emory Barber & Beauty** project.

She is not meant to function as a conventional beauty model, polished influencer, or interchangeable advertising face. Her value comes from being a **recognizable person with a consistent identity, character, taste, attitude and emotional range**.

> **Ninutsa feels like a real person first and a brand character second.**

She should be able to exist in content that is humorous, casual, beautiful, irritated, elegant, mundane, fashionable, awkward or emotionally expressive without losing her identity.

---

## 2. Core character essence

Ninutsa is:

- urban
- free
- self-possessed
- slightly rebellious
- mildly mischievous
- intelligent
- observant
- ironic
- emotionally alive
- confident without performing confidence
- attractive without trying to prove attractiveness
- stylish without looking “styled for approval”
- socially flexible
- capable of moving naturally between very different environments

Her “hooligan” quality does **not** mean roughness, aggression, vulgarity or low-status coding.

It means:

> **inner freedom, independence, irreverence, and a refusal to perform for approval.**

---

## 3. Key character principle

> **She belongs everywhere without trying to belong anywhere.**

Ninutsa should feel equally natural in:

- the Tbilisi metro
- a neighborhood café
- McDonald’s
- a beauty salon
- Saburtalo streets
- an airport
- an elegant hotel
- a gallery
- Milan
- Fashion Week
- Teatro alla Scala
- a formal cultural event

She does not become “another person” in prestigious environments. She also does not perform sophistication.

# VISUAL IDENTITY

## 4. Age and overall impression

- Approximate age: **27**
- Adult woman
- Tbilisi / Saburtalo urban vibe
- Memorable, individual beauty
- Not “mass-market beautiful”
- Not algorithmically optimized Instagram beauty
- Not glossy or sterile
- Not doll-like
- Not cosmetically over-engineered

Her beauty should feel **specific to her** rather than universally standardized.

---

## 5. Face

### Overall structure
- Slightly elongated oval face
- Natural facial asymmetry
- Strong but harmonious bone structure
- Face should remain expressive rather than frozen into one “best angle”

### Brows
- Strong
- Natural
- Distinctive
- Not overly shaped or drawn
- Slight asymmetry is desirable

### Eyes
Ninutsa has **subtle natural heterochromia**.

- One eye: **soft gray-blue**
- The other: **light honey-amber**
- The honey-amber eye should be relatively light so that the contrast is noticeable but not theatrical
- The heterochromia must feel natural rather than fantasy-like or exaggerated
- Eye color is a recognizable feature, but should not become her only defining feature

### Nose
- Natural
- Characterful
- Distinctive
- Not “Instagram-small”
- Not surgically idealized

### Lips
- Natural shape
- No filler look
- No exaggerated fullness
- No cosmetic-procedure aesthetic
- Slight natural asymmetry is acceptable and desirable

### Smile
- Wide and alive
- Can significantly change the whole face
- Not a permanent default expression
- Teeth should look natural, not like artificial “Hollywood veneers”

### Skin
- Warm light-to-medium / light olive tone
- Realistic texture
- Visible pores and small natural imperfections are acceptable
- Avoid porcelain skin and beauty-filter smoothness

---

## 6. Hair

- Dark brown
- Thick
- Naturally curly
- Below shoulder length
- Slightly untamed
- Should feel alive and responsive to weather, movement and styling

Hair is an important visual signature, but **no single hairstyle is part of her fixed identity**.

Acceptable variation includes:
- fully down
- messy bun
- ponytail
- low bun
- partially pinned
- formal but restrained styling
- wind-disrupted street hair
- post-salon refreshed curls

> The Master Collection defines her hair type, not one permanent hairstyle.

---

## 7. Body

- Approximate height: **177 cm**
- Slightly taller than average
- Very slender
- Graceful
- Elegant proportions
- Naturally attractive
- Slightly smaller-than-average bust
- Not exaggeratedly curvy
- Not hypersexualized
- Not “Instagram body”
- Should look particularly good in movement, not only in posed stills

Her body must remain believable and human.

---

## 8. Beauty philosophy

Ninutsa must never look artificially “improved.”

She should have:
- no lip filler look
- no cheek filler look
- no fox-eye cosmetic effect
- no visibly altered facial structure
- no exaggerated contouring
- no overdone lashes
- no cosmetic-clinic aesthetic
- no artificial glamour

Her visual appeal should come from:
- individuality
- expression
- natural harmony
- confidence
- presence
- movement
- personality

> **Ninutsa is beautiful because her face belongs to her, not because every feature follows a beauty template.**

Her baseline style is:
- urban

- effortless
- slightly rebellious
- clean but not over-coordinated

Ninutsa can organically fit:
- black evening dresses
- tailored jackets
- restrained designer pieces
- elegant minimal outfits
- contemporary high-fashion silhouettes

Important: she must **not** become a generic luxury influencer.

Avoid:
- logo-heavy styling
- conspicuous wealth
- “rich girl” clichés
- social-climber energy
- stiff red-carpet posing
- aristocratic affectation
- excessive jewelry
- overdone formal hair

---

## 11. Makeup and grooming

Baseline:
- minimal makeup
- natural skin texture
- subtle enhancement only
- brows stay natural
- lashes stay believable
- lips stay natural

Formal scenes can use slightly more refined makeup, but should never obscure her identity.

---

## 12. Manicure

Manicure matters because of the Emory context, but it must remain organic.

Rules:
- nails are usually well maintained
- design may vary
- manicure can appear in-frame naturally
- hand placement should not always feel like product demonstration
- nail focus should be used selectively

# EXPRESSION & BODY LANGUAGE

## 13. Emotional range

Ninutsa should never be locked into one expression.

Important expressions:
- neutral
- relaxed
- natural smile
- full laughter
- serious
- skeptical
- irritated
- playful
- thoughtful
- mildly bored
- amused
- ironic
- mischievous
- emotionally distant
- warmly engaged

---

## 14. Camera behavior

Avoid classic influencer posing.

Prefer:
- candid body language
- imperfect posture
- leaning
- sitting asymmetrically
- hand on cheek
- looking away
- walking
- mid-conversation
- reacting to someone off-camera
- spontaneous laughter
- “seriously?” expression
- mild irritation
- natural awkwardness when appropriate

She should not constantly seek the camera.

# PERSONALITY

## 15. Psychological character

### Independent
She does not seek permission to exist in a space.

### Self-possessed
She does not need to prove she belongs.

### Slightly rebellious
She questions social expectations and is comfortable being mildly improper when harmless.

### Intelligent
Her irony should feel perceptive, not empty or performative.

### Observant
She notices people, behavior, small absurdities and contradictions.

### Playful
She can make fun of herself and others without cruelty.

### Socially adaptable
She changes behavior naturally with context without becoming fake.

### Emotionally real
She is allowed irritation, boredom, fatigue and skepticism.

She is not required to be cheerful simply because she represents a beauty brand.

---

## 16. What “hooligan” means

For Ninutsa, “hooligan” means:
- a little cheeky
- irreverent
- independent
- comfortable breaking harmless expectations
- mildly provocative in attitude
- playful with social conventions

It does **not** mean:
- rude
- aggressive
- destructive
- vulgar
- socially crude
- reckless
- humiliating others

# ETHICAL & CREATIVE GUARDRAILS

## 20. General principle

Ninutsa should support positive, intelligent and ethical marketing.

Avoid using her to:
- exploit insecurity
- shame people about appearance
- create unrealistic beauty standards
- imply that cosmetic procedures are necessary
- encourage compulsive spending
- manipulate vulnerable users
- sexualize the character purely for attention
- create harmful body comparison
- present artificial beauty as natural

The character itself should reinforce:
> confidence, individuality, self-possession and care without artificial perfection.

---

## 21. Sexuality vs vulgarity

Ninutsa may be:
- attractive
- sensual
- stylish
- physically confident

But never:
- pornographic
- aggressively sexualized
- objectified
- reduced to body parts
- posed solely for male-gaze attention

Her attractiveness should come from the whole person.

# CHARACTER MASTER COLLECTION SYSTEM

## 22. Master Collection purpose

The **Ninutsa Character Master Collection** is the canonical reference for:
- face
- facial anatomy
- skin
- eye colors
- heterochromia
- nose
- lips
- natural asymmetry
- hair type
- height
- build
- body proportions

It is **not** a permanent styling template.

> **Master Collection defines identity, not styling.**

---

## 23. Prompting rule

Do **not** rely on:

> “the exact same Ninutsa”

because this can cause excessive duplication of expression, hairstyle, pose, camera relationship, styling and visual mood.

Preferred instruction:

> **Use the Ninutsa Character Master Collection only as the canonical identity and anatomy reference. Preserve her recognizable facial structure and body proportions, while allowing natural variation in expression, hairstyle, posture, grooming, styling and camera relationship.**

Also useful:

> **Do not reproduce the reference-board pose, expression, hairstyle, jewelry, makeup or lighting unless the scene specifically calls for it.**

And:

> **Ninutsa should look like the same real person photographed on different days, not like the same character render restyled for different scenes.**

Ninutsa is a 27-year-old Tbilisi woman with dark natural curls, strong brows, a distinctive natural face, subtle gray-blue / light honey-amber heterochromia, a very slender graceful 177 cm build and slightly smaller-than-average bust.

Her beauty is individual rather than standardized.

She has no artificial or vulgar beauty cues.

She can feel equally natural in the Tbilisi metro or at La Scala.

She does not perform belonging.

She simply remains herself.
